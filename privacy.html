CLU Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

CLU is a private automation tool used only by its owner, Gordon Crampton, with Google accounts he owns.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, CLU accesses Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive data in the owner's own accounts.

How the data is used

Only to perform tasks the owner requests: answering his questions, preparing email drafts for his review (drafts are never sent automatically), and syncing his calendars.

Storage

CLU runs on the owner's own computer, and sign-in tokens are stored only on that computer. To answer a request, the relevant content may be sent to AI model services the owner uses (such as Anthropic, OpenAI and Google) for processing on his behalf. Nothing is published or stored on a public server.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, or shared with third parties for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
